About Verde Valley Landscaping

Our Story

A Local Crew Built for This Valley

Marco Delgado grew up in Cottonwood, spending summers helping his father tend their backyard garden on the east side of town. What started as weekend work became a lifelong curiosity about plants, water, and how the desert could be both beautiful and livable.

After nearly a decade working for a large Phoenix landscaping firm, Marco came home. In 2014 he founded Verde Valley Landscaping Co. with a single goal: bring sustainable, water-smart landscaping to local homeowners — using plants that truly belong here, designed for the Arizona climate, installed by neighbors who genuinely care about this valley.

Today, our local crew serves hundreds of homeowners across Cottonwood, Sedona, Camp Verde, Clarkdale, and beyond. No subcontractors. No surprises. Just the same team, on every job, every time.

“I want every yard we touch to feel like it belongs here — in Arizona, in this valley, in this neighborhood. Not a transplant from somewhere greener. Something real.”

— Marco Delgado, Founder & Lead Designer
