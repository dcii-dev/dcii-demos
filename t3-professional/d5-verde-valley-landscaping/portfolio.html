Types of Work

What We Build

Our portfolio spans the full range of residential landscaping — from small front yard refreshes to complete property transformations. The most common project types include residential landscape design, which covers full-site plans with plant selection, grading, and phased installation; desert xeriscaping, converting traditional lawns into low-water native plant landscapes; hardscape construction such as flagstone patios, boulder retaining walls, and decomposed granite walkways; irrigation system installation and repair using smart drip and spray technology; and year-round lawn maintenance programs for homeowners who want a consistently polished yard without the labor.

Every project is designed from scratch to fit the property, the climate, and the homeowner’s lifestyle. We don’t use templates — we use site visits.
