Our Specialty

Xeriscaping Specialists for the Verde Valley

Arizona’s desert climate demands landscapes that work with the environment, not against it. Verde Valley Landscaping has completed more than 150 xeriscape projects across Cottonwood, Sedona, and the surrounding communities — each one tailored to the microclimate and soil of the property.

By replacing water-hungry turf with drought-tolerant native plants, decomposed granite, and precision drip irrigation, our xeriscape designs typically reduce outdoor water use by 30–60%. Your yard becomes easier to maintain, more resilient through Arizona summers, and genuinely beautiful year-round.
