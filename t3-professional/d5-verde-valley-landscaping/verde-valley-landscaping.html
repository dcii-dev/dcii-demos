What We Do

Full-Service Landscaping for Every Yard

From concept to completion, we handle every phase of your outdoor project. Whether you need a full redesign or just reliable upkeep, we’ve got you covered.

Landscape Design & Consultation

Sod & Native Plant Installation

From lush sod lawns to low-water native plant beds, we source and install plants that thrive in Arizona’s climate — beautiful year-round with minimal maintenance.

Irrigation & Drip System Installation

Smart irrigation systems designed for Verde Valley conditions. We install, repair, and program drip and spray systems that water efficiently and cut your monthly bill.

Desert Xeriscaping

Dramatically reduce water usage while creating a stunning desert-inspired landscape. We specialize in xeriscape conversions that look incredible and cost less to maintain.

Hardscape Design

Custom patios, flagstone walkways, retaining walls, and outdoor living areas. We blend natural stone, pavers, and concrete into spaces built to last Arizona’s seasons.

Tree & Shrub Trimming

Proper trimming improves health, controls size, and keeps your property safe. We trim native and ornamental trees with care for the plant and your property lines.

Lawn Maintenance Programs

Monthly or bi-weekly maintenance plans that handle mowing, edging, leaf removal, irrigation checks, and seasonal touch-ups. Set it and forget it — we stay on schedule.

Deep seasonal cleanups before and after the harsh summer heat — includes debris removal, bed refresh, plant cutbacks, and fertilization prep to keep your landscape thriving year to year.

Our Story

Rooted in the Verde Valley for Over a Decade

Marco Delgado founded Verde Valley Landscaping Co. in 2014 after years of working for a large Phoenix firm. He came back home to Cottonwood with one goal: build landscapes that truly belong in Arizona — plants that thrive here, designs that respect the desert, and work done by neighbors who genuinely care about this valley.

Today, our local crew handles everything from full landscape installs to weekly maintenance programs across Cottonwood, Sedona, and the surrounding communities. No subcontractors, no shortcuts — just the same team on every job.
