Our Process

How It Works

Five Steps from First Call to Finished Yard

A clear, structured process built around your schedule and your vision — no surprises, no pressure, and no shortcuts on any job we take.

  1. 01

    Free Consultation

    We start with a no-charge, no-obligation site visit at your property. We walk the space together, talk through your goals and budget, and assess the soil, sun exposure, and existing conditions. There’s no sales pitch — just an honest conversation about what’s possible and what it will realistically cost.

    Most consultations take 30 to 60 minutes. We come prepared with questions and leave with enough information to build your custom design plan.

  2. 02

    Design & Planning

    Using what we learned on-site, we develop a full landscape design plan — plant selection, layout, materials, irrigation zones, and phasing. Every element is chosen for the Verde Valley’s specific climate and your property’s conditions.

    We present the design to you with a clear timeline and a line-item written estimate. You know exactly what you’re getting and exactly what it costs before anything begins. No vague quotes, no post-project surprises.

  3. 03

    Material Selection

    Once the design is approved, we source all materials — native plants, stone, gravel, pavers, irrigation components, and soil amendments. We work with trusted local suppliers and nurseries to ensure everything meets our quality standards before it arrives on your property.

    You’re welcome to review and approve material selections before we proceed. We’d rather spend an extra day confirming details than install something that isn’t exactly right.

  4. 04

    Installation

    Our local crew arrives on the scheduled start date, works cleanly, and keeps you updated throughout the job. We respect your property — debris is removed at the end of each day, and we never leave a site in disarray overnight.

    Every installation follows the approved design plan. If we encounter site conditions that affect scope or cost, we stop and discuss before proceeding — never the other way around.

  5. 05

    Final Walkthrough & Care Guide

    Before we consider a job complete, we walk the finished project with you — reviewing every plant, every section of hardscape, and every irrigation zone together. If anything isn’t right, we fix it on the spot.

    You also receive a written care guide specific to your landscape — watering schedules, seasonal maintenance notes, and plant-by-plant instructions so you know exactly how to keep everything thriving. And if you’d rather hand that off to us, we offer year-round maintenance programs.

FAQ

Common Questions

Still have questions? Call us at (928) 555-0247 or send us a message — we’re happy to help.

How long does a typical project take?

Timeline depends on the scope. A standard xeriscape conversion or native plant installation typically takes 3–7 business days. Hardscape projects like patios or retaining walls range from 5–14 days. Full landscape redesigns may run 2–4 weeks depending on property size and material delivery schedules. We provide a project timeline in your written estimate before any work begins — so there are no scheduling surprises.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes — every estimate is completely free and comes with no obligation. We schedule a site visit, walk the property with you, discuss your goals and budget, and follow up within 48 hours with a written, line-item estimate. There’s no pressure and no charge for the consultation or the estimate itself.

What plants work best in the Verde Valley?

The Verde Valley’s high desert climate at 3,300–4,500 feet supports a wide range of native and adapted plants. Our top performers include Desert Willow, Blue Palo Verde, Autumn Sage, Agave, Desert Marigold, Apache Plume, and Trailing Lantana. We select plants based on your specific site conditions — sun exposure, soil type, and water availability — so every plant we install is set up to thrive in your yard, not just survive.

Do you handle permits?

For most residential landscaping — plant installation, xeriscape, irrigation, and seasonal cleanup — permits are not required. For certain hardscape projects, such as retaining walls above a specific height or structures in regulated setback areas, we handle the permit application on your behalf as part of the project scope. We assess permit needs during the design phase and communicate any requirements clearly before work begins.

What’s your warranty on installation work?

We stand behind our work. Plant material is warranted for 90 days from installation — if a plant doesn’t establish, we replace it at no charge. Irrigation systems are warranted for 1 year against workmanship defects. Hardscape installations including patios, walls, and walkways carry a 2-year workmanship warranty. We’re a local company — we stand by our work because we live here too.
