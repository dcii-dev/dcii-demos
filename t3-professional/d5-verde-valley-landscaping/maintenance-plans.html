Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What’s included in a maintenance visit?

Every visit includes mowing, edging, and blowing, plus a visual inspection of plants and irrigation. Depending on your plan, visits may also include fertilization, irrigation adjustments, weed management, and seasonal cleanup work.

Can I customize my plan?

Yes. We can add or remove specific services from any base plan. Tell us what matters most for your property and we’ll put together a custom scope that fits your yard and your budget.

Do you service all Verde Valley cities?

Yes. We service all Verde Valley communities including Cottonwood, Sedona, Camp Verde, Clarkdale, Jerome, Cornville, Rimrock, Village of Oak Creek, and Beaver Creek.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can cancel your maintenance plan at any time with 30 days’ written notice. No multi-year contracts, no cancellation fees. We earn your continued business by showing up and doing good work.

Do maintenance plans include plant replacement?

Standard plant replacement is not included in maintenance plans. However, Signature and Premium clients receive a priority rate on replacement plants sourced and installed by our own crew. Premium clients also receive an annual planting refresh of up to 6 plants per year.
