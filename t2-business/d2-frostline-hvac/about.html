About Frostline HVAC

Our Story

Built on Honesty.
Grown on Trust.

Frostline was founded in 2009 by Derek Callahan with nothing more than two trucks, a set of tools, and a deep frustration with how homeowners were being treated by HVAC companies. He had spent years watching customers get hit with hidden fees, vague estimates, and repeat calls for the same unfixed problem.

Today, Frostline has grown into a team of 18+ NATE-certified technicians serving the greater Denver metro. The mission hasn’t changed: flat-rate pricing, no-surprise billing, and a commitment to doing the job right the first time.

“I started Frostline because I was tired of seeing homeowners get overcharged and under-served. We built this company around one idea: do it right the first time.”

— Derek Callahan, Owner & Master Technician
