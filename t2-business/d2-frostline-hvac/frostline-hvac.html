Our Story

Denver-Owned. Denver-Operated.
Since 2009.

Frostline was founded by Marcus and Elena Vargas after Marcus spent a decade working for large HVAC corporations and grew frustrated watching homeowners get overcharged for simple repairs.

Today, Frostline operates 14 fully stocked service trucks across the Denver metro — staffed by NATE-certified technicians who live in the same neighborhoods they serve.

Our flat-rate pricing model, written satisfaction guarantee, and no-commission technicians mean you get honest service every time — not an upsell.
