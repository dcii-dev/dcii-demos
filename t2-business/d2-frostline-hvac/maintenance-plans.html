Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What’s included in a tune-up?

Every tune-up includes our 18-point system inspection: we check refrigerant levels, test electrical connections, inspect the heat exchanger for cracks, clean the evaporator and condenser coils, lubricate moving parts, calibrate your thermostat, and replace your air filter — all at no extra charge. You receive a written report after every visit.

How often should I schedule maintenance?

Twice per year is ideal — once in spring before cooling season, and once in fall before heating season. Silver plan members receive one annual visit; Gold and Platinum members receive both seasonal visits automatically with no scheduling needed on your end.

Will a maintenance plan void my equipment warranty?

No — quite the opposite. Most manufacturer warranties require annual professional maintenance to remain valid. Skipping maintenance is one of the most common reasons warranty claims are denied. Our dated, signed service records document every visit and protect your coverage.

What about emergency service?

All plan members receive priority dispatch on emergency calls — meaning you move ahead of non-plan customers during busy periods. Gold members receive after-hours appointment priority. Platinum members receive front-of-line emergency dispatch and our no-overtime-charge guarantee, any day, any hour.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. All plans are billed annually, but you can cancel with 30 days’ written notice — no cancellation fees and no penalties. If you cancel mid-year, we’ll prorate your remaining balance and issue a refund within 10 business days.
