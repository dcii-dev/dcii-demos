Common Questions

Financing FAQs

Straight answers to the questions we hear most often about roofing financing.

What credit score do I need to qualify?

Credit requirements vary by lender. GreenSky and Synchrony HOME generally require a minimum score of 580–620 for their promotional plans, though qualifying terms improve with higher scores. Our in-house payment plans have no credit requirement — they're available to any customer regardless of credit history.

How quickly will I know if I'm approved?

GreenSky and Synchrony HOME both offer near-instant decisions — most applicants receive a determination within 2–5 minutes of completing the application. Our in-house plans are arranged directly with our team and are typically confirmed at the time of your estimate appointment.

How do I apply for financing?

The easiest path is to schedule a free inspection first. Once we've assessed your roof and provided a written estimate, our project coordinator will walk you through the available financing options and help you start the application — either on-site or via a secure link sent to your phone or email. Applications typically take under 10 minutes.

What does "same-as-cash" actually mean?

A same-as-cash plan — like the Synchrony HOME 12-month option — means you pay zero interest if you pay off the full balance within the promotional period. It works like an interest-free loan for that window. If the balance isn't paid in full by the end of the promotional period, deferred interest calculated from the original purchase date is added to the remaining balance. We always explain this clearly before you sign anything.

Can I finance only part of my project?

Yes. Financing doesn't have to cover the entire project cost. Many customers prefer to pay a larger down payment and finance a smaller portion, or split a project into phases and finance only the current phase. We're flexible — tell us what you're comfortable with and we'll structure the plan around your budget.
