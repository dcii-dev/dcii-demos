About Peakstone Roofing

A family-owned roofing company born and raised in Cedar Valley, PA. We've protected homes here for over 15 years — and we plan to keep doing it for 15 more.

Our Story

Cedar Valley Built, Cedar Valley Proud

Peakstone Roofing was founded right here in Cedar Valley with a simple idea: do honest work, charge a fair price, and stand behind every job we touch. Over the past 15 years, that idea has turned into hundreds of completed projects and thousands of homeowners who sleep better knowing their roofs were done right.

We're a family-owned business — not a franchise, not a national chain. When you call us, you talk to the people who will actually show up and do the work. Our owner is on every job site, not behind a desk, and our crew has been with us for years because we treat them the same way we treat our customers.

We handle both residential and commercial projects — from a single-family repair to a full commercial tear-off. Every job gets the same level of craftsmanship, the same materials, and the same follow-through. That's not a slogan. It's how we've built our reputation in this community.

Meet the Team

The People Behind Your Roof

Our crew has been together for years. When we show up to your home, you're getting experienced people — not day laborers hired off the street.

Marcus Hale

Owner & Lead Roofer

Marcus started Peakstone in 2010 after spending nearly a decade learning the trade from the ground up. A Cedar Valley native, he wanted to build something his community could depend on — a roofing company that said what it meant and delivered what it promised.

He holds GAF Master Elite and CertainTeed SELECT ShingleMaster certifications, and is on the job site for every major project. When he's not on a roof, he's coaching his son's youth baseball team in Hillcrest.
